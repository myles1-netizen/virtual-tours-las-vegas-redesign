# VTLV FAQs

**Source URL:** https://virtualtourslasvegas.com/about/vtlv-faqs/
**Page Title:** "FAQ's for 3D Virtual Interactive Tours - Matterport Las Vegas 360 Real Estate Photos"

## FAQ Content (as extracted — quoted portions are verbatim per the extractor; unquoted portions are paraphrase and should be spot-checked)

**Q: How is a 360 Virtual Tour different from a Traditional Virtual Tour?**
A: Traditional virtual tours are "merely a gallery of stationary 2D photos made to look panoramic" with limited viewing locations. VTLV's 360 tours allow exploration of "up to 200 locations that are seamlessly stitched together as photo spheres" using Matterport technology. Creates floor plans and 3D models viewable online or with VR headsets, with "Floor Plan View" and "Dollhouse View" options. Exterior HDR/twilight photography available separately.

**Q: How long does it take to scan a typical space?**
A: "It typically takes 30-40 minutes for every 1,000 square feet. Each individual scan takes about 1 minute and some homes can use up to 200 scans."

**Q: What do I need to do before the appointment?**
A: No physical items required; spaces should be "in a condition suitable for scanning" — clean/staged as for an open house. Photographer won't spend appointment time arranging lights or furniture. Properties not ready or inaccessible incur a $35 trip charge and rescheduling.

**Q: What if the weather is bad on the day of my appointment?**
A: HDR camera "allows the camera to adjust to changes in light so both light and dark areas can be captured." Interior scanning works on cloudy days with proper lighting; outdoor scans not guaranteed.

**Q: What do I need to do during the appointment?**
A: Client is "not required to be on site while I scan the property." Scanning typically 1-3 hours; "all pets and humans are removed from the property during scanning" is requested.

**Q: Which Browsers are compatible with the 3D Virtual Tour?**
A: "The 3D Virtual Tour uses WebGL, the modern standard for 3D content viewed on the web." Compatible: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Internet Explorer (desktop); Safari for iOS 8, Chrome for Android (mobile).

**Q: What's the difference between a Schematic Floor Plan and a Blueprint?**
A: "Schematic floor plans are more like maps that are not necessarily drawn to scale. Blueprints on the other hand are fully-scaled drawings of the actual building layout using surveying tools and techniques." Blueprints include cross-sections, footing, framing, roofing, electrical, plumbing details for construction.

**Q: Aren't schematic floor plans just what architects use to show clients before creating a more formal blueprint?**
A: "In some cases, yes. However, the schematic floor plans provided by Virtual Tours Las Vegas are limited specifically to existing construction, not to new or add-on construction planning."

**Q: How can I use a floor plan for an As-Built Survey or Remodel of my property?**
A: Suggests creating a Matterport virtual tour to generate "Matterpack BIM modeling" that "integrates with software like SketchUp, Revit, AutoCad and other BIM Software."

**Q: How long is my 3D Virtual Tour hosted online?**
A: "The first 6 months of hosting for your virtual tour is included in your original scan/tour pricing and is only $10/month per model annually (billed in 6 month increments) after that. You are free to cancel at anytime."

**Q: What is your turn around time?**
A: Still photography: 24-48 hours via cloud. 3D Virtual Tours: 24-48 hours via email links. Floor plans: 48 hours as PDF. "Zillow Walk Through Videos: 12-24 hours."

**Q: What is your cancelation policy?**
A: "You must contact me at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment time in order to avoid a $35 trip charge."
